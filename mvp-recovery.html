MVP Recovery

Technical debt is a silent growth killer. We diagnose the root cause and rebuild the foundation your next phase demands — without burning everything down.

Diagnose Your Product →

Your product got you here. It will not get you there.

Early-stage products are built for speed. That is rational. But the shortcuts that helped you launch — the hardcoded values, the tightly coupled services, the missing test coverage — become anchors the moment you try to scale.

MVP Recovery is a focused intervention. We audit your existing codebase, infrastructure, and user experience to surface what is actually holding growth back. Then we rebuild the critical foundations — surgically, without the disruption of a full rewrite.

The goal is not perfection. It is removing the ceiling on your next growth phase while keeping the product live and your team productive throughout.

Recognise Any of These?

Signs your product needs recovery — not iteration.

Performance

Load times that bleed users before they convert

Every second of latency costs measurable revenue. Slow core flows are masking the full damage to your retention numbers.

Stability

Outages and fires that consume your team

If engineering spends more time on incident response than shipping, the codebase architecture is the problem — not the people.

Velocity

Simple changes take weeks and break things

When every feature touches everything else and deployments feel like gambling, development velocity compounds against you.

UX

An interface that leaks users at every step

If users drop out of flows that should work, the problem is rarely the user — it is friction baked into the product experience.

Scale

Infrastructure that groans under demand

A spike in traffic should not be a crisis. If load events expose structural limits, you are one viral moment away from an outage at exactly the wrong time.

Security

Exposure that would end you in an audit or breach

Early-stage shortcuts become tomorrow's liability as you grow — and attract more scrutiny.

How We Work

A structured path from fragile to formidable.

01

Deep Audit

We spend the first week inside your codebase, infrastructure, and product analytics. The outcome is a prioritised list of what is causing the most damage — ranked by impact, not by what is easiest to fix.

02

Triage & Roadmap

Not everything needs fixing on day one. We work with you to agree a sequenced roadmap that recovers the highest-leverage foundations first, minimising disruption to your live product throughout.

03

Surgical Rebuild

We execute the roadmap in focused sprints. Where possible, we refactor rather than rewrite — preserving business logic while replacing the structural problems. Where a full rebuild is necessary, we do it in parallel with the live product.

04

Validation & Handover

Every recovery engagement ends with comprehensive testing, performance benchmarking, and a structured handover. You leave with documentation, improved tooling, and a team that understands the new architecture.

What Changes

Measurable results — not abstract improvements.

Faster page and API response times that directly improve conversion and retention
A codebase your engineers can move in — features in days, not weeks
Infrastructure that scales predictably under load without manual intervention
Fewer incidents and outages — and faster resolution when they do occur
UX flows that guide users to conversion rather than leaking them at every step
Security posture that withstands scrutiny from enterprise buyers and auditors
